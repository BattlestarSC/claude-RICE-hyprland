Launcher Concepts

Six directions in the Quasar palette, combining your Rim pick, the retro TUI menu and Aylur's "Globes" (Hyprland Hall of Fame). Concepts 1–3 have no retro influence; 4–6 go from strong to light. All have icons. The wallpaper behind each mock, the panel starfields and the planet are real output of tools/spacegen.py in this palette.

The engine is the real decision. rofi covers 1, 2, 5, 6 and an approximate 4. Concept 3 and a faithful 4 need Quickshell (the retro menu you showed appears to be Quickshell; it is in that screenshot's process list). Choosing Quickshell for the launcher means the bar is built in the same QML codebase, so those two decisions merge. rofi -dmenu stays the engine for scripted menus either way. Contrast in all mocks: body text 14.2:1 / Lc 88 on mantle; dark text on the red pill 8.7:1.

Generator output: planet, panel starfield, wallpaper (seeded, so reproducible).
