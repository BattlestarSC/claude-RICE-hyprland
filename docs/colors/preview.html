Rice Palette Proposals

Color schemes for the Hyprland + Kitty + Zsh rice. All share Monokai's role mapping (keywords red, functions green, strings yellow, numbers purple, builtins cyan, parameters orange) on a dark, blue-violet "space" base. Round 2 is Quasar, a blend between round 1's Pulsar and Event Horizon, plus a dial to pick your own point on that axis. The mockups use the real hex values.

Two contrast measures. WCAG ratio (x:1) is the accessibility baseline. APCA Lc is perceptual and polarity-aware, so it catches what WCAG misses on dark themes: glare from text that is too bright (Lc above ~92) and uneven accents. Accent spread is the gap in Lc between the brightest and dimmest accent. Pulsar's 36 is why its yellow glares while its red and purple recede.

Quasar

Pulsar's dark base and Monokai hues, Event Horizon's softer text, accents evened out in perceived lightness.
