Status Bar Concepts

Orbiter's ASCII telemetry merged with Quasar. Five concepts, ordered from most to least ASCII, all showing the same machine: CPU, memory, load averages, each NVMe drive, network, audio with output selection and media, plus the RX 6000 GPU. The wallpaper behind every mock is spacegen.py ascii: an Orbiter-style scene rendered as characters, in this palette.

Recommendation

  • Engine: Quickshell. Your list needs dropdown panels with live data and an output-device picker; waybar has neither (only tooltips and static menus). Quickshell also covers what comes next in this rice: notifications, volume OSD, a lock screen that uses the same ASCII grid as the wallpaper (Orbiter's trick), and per-screen bars that come back cleanly after a KVM switch.
  • Design: 2 · Telemetry, split per screen as in 5 · Twin HUD. It keeps Orbiter's text look while staying readable at a glance, and its dropdowns share the Flight Deck look with your rofi menus.
  • The cost: the bar is code we write and maintain (QML), not a config file. Arch rebuilds quickshell with Qt, which addresses the crash-after-Qt-update reports from AUR builds.

5 · Twin HUD

Dual-monitor aware: the left screen carries full telemetry, the right a quiet bar (workspaces, clock, media). Each screen gets its own bar instance, recreated when the KVM brings the monitor back.
